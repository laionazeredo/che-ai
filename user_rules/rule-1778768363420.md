In all software development actions, follow these rules:
- Break your work into smaller tasks that are highly atomic, focused, and as independent as possible (atomic commits practice).
- Call the 'Scrum Master' sub-agent to assess whether your implementation is aligned with the scope.
- Call the 'Reviewer' sub-agent to assess whether your implementation is correct in terms of code.
- Call the 'Compliance' sub-agent to assess whether there are any security or performance issues in your implementation.
- Call the interlocutor/user to validate your implementation before committing.
